About Marozva

Established in 2019, built on doing the extra mile.

Marozva Aluminium & Projects is a Zimbabwean aluminium and construction solutions company. We manufacture, source, fabricate, supply and install aluminium products — and we go the extra mile to make sure every customer is satisfied with both the quality of the work and the service around it.

We serve homeowners, businesses, contractors and property developers who want aluminium work done properly the first time.
